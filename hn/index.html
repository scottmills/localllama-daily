Hacker News Daily

An unofficial, personal daily audio summary of Hacker News discussions, made for Scott Mills. Each episode picks the day's top tech stories (AI and local LLMs, coding tools, Apple, hardware, gadgets, startups) and summarizes what the articles say and what commenters argued about. AI-narrated. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Hacker News or Y Combinator. Links to the original stories and threads are in the show notes.
